## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Tone
The design system reflects an institutional, prestigious, and panafrican authority representing craftsmanship and technical mastery. It blends sovereign legitimacy with entrepreneurial dynamism. The tone is stately yet accessible, exuding reliability, civic pride, craftsmanship rigor, and modern progress.

### Visual Style
A tailored blend of **Contemporary Institutionalism** and **Tactile Prestige**:
- Crisp, hyper-legible geometric typography for executive authority and digital service clarity.
- Balanced structural layout balancing governmental solemnity with the vitality of independent artisans.
- Polished micro-accents: refined gold hair-lines, republic-inspired seals, certified craft validation badges, and high-clarity transactional focus.
- Avoids cold bureaucracy by integrating warm, material-inspired amber undertones and vibrant growth green indicators.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
- **Primary (`#0C2340`) — Bleu Républicain Souverain**: Anchors executive headers, navigational shells, authoritative typography, and deep background accents. Conveying stability, state trust, and institutional heritage.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`) — Ambre Artisanal & Or Prestige**: Highlights craftsmanship excellence, verified seals, master technician distinctions, and decisive transactional calls to action (`Devenir membre`).
- **Tertiary (`#059669`) — Vert Émergence & Vitalité**: Signifies institutional verification, certified credentials, sustainable economic emergence, and active member status.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#0F172A`)**: Base surfaces utilize crisp pearl white (`#F8FAFC`) and layered mist containers (`#F1F5F9`), paired with deep obsidian slate (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast, accessible typography conforming to WCAG AAA institutional standards.

### Functional Tones
- **Subtle Gold Rim**: 1px borders using `#D97706` at 20% opacity for certified member profiles and state credentials.
- **Surface Elevation**: Tinted ambient surface fills in `#FFFFFF` sitting atop `#F1F5F9` canvas bases.

## Typography

### Structural Decisions
- **Unified Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans is deployed across headlines, body, and UI metadata. Its geometric modernism provides executive precision without the cold sterile feeling of traditional administrative grotesques.
- **Hierarchical Discipline**: Displays and headlines use tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a sculpted, authoritative architectural cadence.
- **Accreditation Labels & Badges**: Small functional labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use uppercase transformations paired with generous tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to echo state diplomas, registration numbers, and official trade seals.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1360px max bounded container width; 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters; 48px (`3rem`) lateral margins. Keeps official records, guild directories, and ministerial notices balanced and centered.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Fluid breakpoint between 768px and 1024px; 20px gutters; 32px lateral margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Fluid under 768px; 16px (`1rem`) gutters; 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
- Internal component groupings adhere strictly to a 4px/8px incremental scale.
- Hero banners and official declarations utilize expansive `space-xl` breathing room to project state prestige.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Model
The visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering** coupled with **Subtle Tinted Shadows** and **Gold Hairline Frameworks**:

1. **Base Foundation (Level 0)**: `#F8FAFC` flat canvas for document backgrounds and structural viewports.
2. **Elevated Containers (Level 1)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-diffused sovereign shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(12, 35, 64, 0.05)`.
3. **Accredited & Featured Cards (Level 2)**: Crisp card surfaces ringed with a dual border: an inner 1px border of `#D97706` (20% opacity) and an ambient elevation: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(12, 35, 64, 0.08)`.
4. **Modal Panels & Portals (Level 3)**: Espace Membres popovers and search dialogs leverage `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(12, 35, 64, 0.16)` with subtle backdrop blur (`12px`) across deep navigation bars.

## Shapes

### Form Language
- Roundedness level **2** (`8px` / `0.5rem` base, `16px` / `1rem` on `rounded-lg`) provides an institutional balance: neither aggressively brutalist nor casually hyper-rounded.
- **Primary Structural Elements**: Cards, input fields, and member dossier panels use `0.5rem` to project structured discipline.
- **Action Pills & Badges**: Registered certifications, national status indicators, and search filters incorporate `rounded-lg` or full pill geometry (`9999px`) to create clear tactile contrast against geometric cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero CTA ("Devenir membre")**: Solid `#D97706` amber fill with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` typography. Hover transitions to `#B45309` with a subtle elevation shift (`0 4px 12px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Sovereign CTA ("Trouver un artisan")**: Deep `#0C2340` republican blue background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#1B365D`.
- **Portal Direct Access ("Espace Membres")**: Translucent pearl fill (`#FFFFFF`), 1.5px border of `#0C2340`, and `#0C2340` bold typography; displays a mini gold key or lock icon.

### Form Inputs
- **Artisan Search & Registry Filter**: 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Focused state transitions border to `#0C2340` with an outer ring in `#0C2340` at 15% opacity.
- **Placeholder**: Subdued slate `#64748B`.

### Certification Badges & Chips
- **Certified Artisan Chip**: Soft emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), solid emerald border (`#A7F3D0`), and `#065F46` label accompanied by a certified checkmark icon.
- **Chamber Seal Tag**: `#FFFBEB` gold tint background, `#FDE68A` border, and `#92400E` text for national trade register markers.

### Artisan & Member Dossier Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` container surfaces, 16px padding, and 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- Top header features the artisan’s official registration number (`Matricule ATAC`) rendered in `label-sm` with a sovereign blue font.
- Bottom card actions host dual split actions: "Contacter" (ghost) and "Voir Profil Officiel" (secondary button).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square checkbox with `4px` corner radius. Checked fill in `#0C2340` with a sharp white check glyph.
- Radio buttons feature a concentric `#D97706` inner dot indicator.
